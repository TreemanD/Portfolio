Artifact Definition

I created this job aid as part of my graduate studies in the Instructional Systems Technology program at Indiana University. The course artifact applies to my current job and provides rules for creating an audience. There is the potential where an LMS Administrator may have a three month gap between when initially learning these rules to when they need to apply it, so I created this job aid as a quick reference for that situation. I used Adobe Illustrator to create this job aid.
