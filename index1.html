Welcome!

My name is John Holbrook. I specialize in applying educational technology to optimize knowledge transfer and learner outcomes. I am an e-learning and learning technology professional with more than 16 years of experience supporting learning technologies and over 10 years of experience developing training content and technical documentation. My work has included learning management system administration, learning technology support, reporting and analytics, process improvement, and the development of digital learning resources.

Career Goal

My career goal is to build on my experience in learning management systems, learning technologies, instructional design, and learning analytics to help organizations create effective and meaningful learning experiences. I want to continue expanding my knowledge of emerging learning technologies while strengthening my ability to use analytics and reporting to evaluate and improve learning initiatives. Through my professional experience and graduate studies in Instructional Systems Technology, I also hope to develop my expertise in needs analysis and apply it to increasingly strategic learning and development roles, with the long-term goal of leading a training or learning organization.
